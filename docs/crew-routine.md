# HARDBOND daily and weekly routine

Open **Crew & routine → How to use** for these instructions inside the app.

## Set up once

1. **Task cards:** load the 35 source cards. B1 retains its original owner-FINAL label, but operational approval is separate. Review the cards you will actually use against equipment, manufacturer instructions, safety requirements and confirmed trailer locations. Save corrections first; then enter the reviewer and approve the saved revision. Unreviewed cards cannot be shared in briefs.
2. **Trailer checks:** expand the proposed map. Confirm the layout physically, label shelves/boxes, and take shelf reference photos. In **Inventory**, enter actual storage locations, usable counts and minimum stock. Record photo references in notes. The map does not establish product storage suitability or trailer load capacity.
3. **Workers:** add real names, contacts, transport and availability. Assess each relevant task as SOLO, ASSIST, NEVER or NOT ASSESSED. A lead/experienced role does not grant task clearance automatically.
4. **Jobs:** check addresses, arrival times, system and material requirements. Use existing records rather than creating duplicates from calendar events.

## Every evening: prepare tomorrow

1. Read the latest **Daily Sheet** and open **Action list** items.
2. Open **Crew & routine → Day plan**, choose tomorrow's date and enter the lead contact.
3. Add the actual workers. Confirm their availability, arrival time, meeting point and transport.
4. Add presets or individual tasks for each job. Adjust sequence and describe timing, work area and travel. Shared prerequisites must be coordinated by the lead; presets are starting points, not an automatic job method.
5. For ASSIST tasks, select an attending supervisor with SOLO clearance and confirm they are qualified and available at that job without conflicting duties.
6. Save the day plan. Resolve warnings before sharing.
7. Review each worker's full brief. Check that it contains no private information or money. Copy it into your normal messaging app, send it, then mark it sent.
8. Record confirmation only after the worker replies. A changed plan needs new briefs and confirmations. There is no automatic SMS/WhatsApp delivery or reply monitoring.
9. Use **Load checks** to physically count the actual job load. Resolve shortages before departure.

## Every morning

1. Open **Overview → Crew readiness**. Resolve missing plans, unconfirmed workers, changed briefs and clearance problems.
2. Check transport, the physical load, access and job readiness. Resolve safety issues before mobilizing.
3. On site, review actual conditions and task instructions with the crew.
4. Use **Field plan** checks. Save the completed phase and resolved checks first; then enter the lead who inspected and released it, and save again. Editing a phase clears its release. A software record is not an on-site inspection.

## During work and before leaving

1. Record conditions, holds and defects in the existing Field plan / Daily Sheet. Follow stop-and-ask instructions.
2. In **Day plan**, mark assignments Done, Partial or Not done. Explain unfinished work, then save. These same records appear in **History**.
3. Complete the job's **Daily Sheet** with actual hours, materials, issues and tomorrow's needs. Reviewing the sheet applies recorded material usage; do not also subtract that usage manually.
4. Complete **Trailer checks → End-of-day return**. Return equipment, label identifiable partial kits, separate dirty tools, and isolate damaged equipment.
5. Update Inventory for actual physical counts when needed. Put shortages or repairs in **Action list** with an owner and due date. Checklist notes do not automatically create purchase orders or update stock.
6. Confirm queued offline edits have synced before relying on shared plans. Refresh after sync before sharing/confirming briefs.

## Every week

1. Complete **Trailer checks → Weekly audit**. Count usable stock, check minimums, inspect equipment and resolve purchase/repair actions.
2. Review upcoming jobs, worker availability, transport and required cards.
3. Review **History** for incomplete work or recurring problems. Coach workers and deliberately reassess clearances; completion does not automatically promote a worker.
4. Improve cards that caused confusion. Edited cards require approval again; changed briefs require resending.
5. Export a backup in **Settings**, and check pending offline edits.

## What stays manual

- Actual worker availability, qualifications, supervision coverage and receipt of messages.
- Physical load and trailer inspections, stock counts and quality checks.
- Product-specific technical decisions and approval of source drafts.
- Existing TimeTree connectivity: this release does not add authenticated TimeTree scraping.

Briefs deliberately omit job titles, private job notes and worker coaching notes. A money-reference check catches common pricing text, but you must still review free-text instructions before sharing. Workers receive the copied brief; do not give them the owner login.
